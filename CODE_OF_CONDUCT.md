# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to make the project better.

## The standard

Behaviour that helps:

- Reviewing the change rather than the person.
- Saying what is wrong and what would be right, in the same message.
- Accepting that a maintainer may say no to a good idea because of scope.
- Writing down reasoning, so the next person does not have to reconstruct it.

Behaviour that does not:

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Deliberately wasting other people's time.
- Dismissiveness towards beginners.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, the issue tracker, pull requests, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainer through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/EmmiSiu/rincon/security/advisories/new) or by a
direct message. Reports are handled privately. Consequences range from a warning to a permanent
ban, proportionate to what happened.

Maintainers who do not act in good faith on a report face the same consequences.
